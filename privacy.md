# Privacy Policy

Discover (https://discoverall.space) is a non-commercial link-sharing platform. **We do not collect personal data.** We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or IP address. We do not use cookies.

We record only anonymous, aggregate statistics — view counts, likes, dislikes, and the country/platform of visits. This data cannot identify you and is **not personal data** under India’s DPDP Act, 2023.

We do not sell or share any data. We use these statistics solely to operate the site.

For any privacy question, please write to: **https://github.com/abirusabil123/discover/issues**.
